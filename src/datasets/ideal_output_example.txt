Here’s a **time-efficient, bodyweight-focused weekly workout routine** tailored to your goal of **building muscle** with limited gym access.
This plan emphasizes **compound movements**, **progressive overload**, and **muscle engagement** through advanced bodyweight techniques.
Each session is ~45–60 minutes, and you can adjust intensity as you progress.

---

### **Weekly Workout Split**  
**Days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday (rest on Saturday/Sunday or active recovery)  
**Focus:** Upper body push, upper body pull, lower body, full body, and active recovery.

---

### **Day 1: Upper Body Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)**  
**Warm-up (5–10 min):** Jumping jacks, arm circles, shoulder rolls, dynamic push-up position stretches.  

**Workout:**  
1. **Push-ups (variations as needed):**  
   - 4 sets of 10–15 reps (modify with knees or incline if needed; advance to decline or explosive push-ups).  
   - Rest 30–60 seconds between sets.  
2. **Tricep Dips (on bench or chair):**  
   - 3 sets of 10–15 reps.  
   - Rest 30 seconds.  
3. **Shoulder Tap Push-ups:**  
   - 3 sets of 12–15 reps per side (tap opposite shoulder while in push-up position).  
   - Rest 30 seconds.  
4. **Plank to Downward Dog (core + shoulders):**  
   - 3 sets of 12–15 reps.  

**Cool-down (5–10 min):** Stretch chest, shoulders, and triceps.  

---

### **Day 2: Upper Body Pull (Back, Biceps, Rear Delts)**  
**Warm-up (5–10 min):** Arm swings, cat-cow stretch, lat stretches.  

**Workout:**  
1. **Bodyweight Rows (using a pull-up bar or resistance band):**  
   - 4 sets of 8–12 reps (focus on squeezing shoulder blades).  
   - Rest 45 seconds.  
2. **Inverted Rows (using a table or low bar):**  
   - 3 sets of 10–15 reps.  
   - Rest 30 seconds.  
3. **Chin-ups (if possible):**  
   - 3 sets of 6–10 reps.  
   - Rest 45 seconds.  
4. **YTWL Shoulder Stretch (for rear delts):**  
   - 3 sets of 12–15 reps (hold each letter for 2–3 seconds).  

**Cool-down (5–10 min):** Stretch back, biceps, and lats.  

---

### **Day 3: Lower Body (Quads, Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves)**  
**Warm-up (5–10 min):** Leg swings, hip circles, lunges.  

**Workout:**  
1. **Bodyweight Squats (advanced: jump squats, pistol squats):**  
   - 4 sets of 12–15 reps.  
   - Rest 30–60 seconds.  
2. **Glute Bridges (with leg lift for added difficulty):**  
   - 3 sets of 15–20 reps per leg.  
   - Rest 30 seconds.  
3. **Lunges (walking or stationary):**  
   - 3 sets of 12–15 reps per leg.  
   - Rest 30 seconds.  
4. **Calf Raises (on stairs or elevated surface):**  
   - 3 sets of 15–20 reps.  

**Cool-down (5–10 min):** Stretch quads, hamstrings, and calves.  

---

### **Day 4: Full Body (Compound Movements + Core)**  
**Warm-up (5–10 min):** Jumping jacks, dynamic stretches.  

**Workout:**  
1. **Burpees (modified if needed):**  
   - 3 sets of 8–12 reps.  
   - Rest 45 seconds.  
2. **Mountain Climbers (for core + cardio):**  
   - 3 sets of 20–30 reps per side.  
   - Rest 30 seconds.  
3. **Push-up + Squat Combo (super-set):**  
   - 3 sets of 10–12 reps (do a push-up, then a squat, alternating).  
   - Rest 45 seconds.  
4. **Plank (with leg lifts or side planks):**  
   - 3 sets of 30–60 seconds.  

**Cool-down (5–10 min):** Foam roll and stretch major muscle groups.  

---

### **Day 5: Active Recovery or Light Mobility**  
- **Walk or yoga (30–45 min)**  
- **Stretching:** Focus on tight areas (hamstrings, shoulders, hips).  
- **Foam rolling:** 10–15 minutes.  

---

### **Tips for Muscle Growth**  
1. **Progressive Overload:** Increase reps, decrease rest time, or add advanced variations (e.g., decline push-ups, weighted vest for squats).  
2. **Nutrition:** Prioritize **protein** (1.6–2.2g/kg body weight) and a **calorie surplus** (eat more than you burn).  
3. **Sleep:** Aim for 7–9 hours/night for recovery.  
4. **Track Progress:** Take weekly photos, measure circumference, and note strength gains.  

---

### **Sample Meal Snack Ideas**  
- **Protein sources:** Eggs, chicken, fish, tofu, lentils, Greek yogurt.  
- **Carbs:** Brown rice, oats, sweet potatoes, fruits.  
- **Fats:** Nuts, avocado, olive oil.  

---

This plan balances **muscle-building intensity** with **time efficiency**. As you get stronger, increase difficulty (e.g., add resistance, slow tempo, or isometric holds).
